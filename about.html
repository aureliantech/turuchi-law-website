— My story

From defending insurers to representing the injured.

I began my career on the defense side — representing insurance companies, the corporations they protect, and the institutions that often outspend and outlast the people they harm. I learned the playbook from inside. I saw which cases got settled, which got fought, and which got pushed aside.

What I also saw was the kind of person who lost most often: the injured worker who didn't know they had rights. The immigrant who was told their case wasn't worth anything. The driver whose lawyer stopped returning calls. Good people, fighting a system designed to wear them down.

I built this firm to be the answer to that. A practice where every client is taken seriously, every case is built carefully, and every decision is explained clearly — by a lawyer, in plain language, every step of the way.

— How I work

My approach to every case.

  1. i.

    I take the cases other firms turn down.

    If you were told your case is not worth it, that is exactly when I want to hear from you. Some of my best results came from cases that looked small at first.

  2. ii.

    I prepare every file like it's going to trial.

    Most cases end in a settlement — that's normal. But getting ready for court is what drives that number up. The insurance company pays more when it sees a lawyer who is ready to fight in front of a judge.

  3. iii.

    I explain everything in plain language.

    You'll never wonder where your case stands or what's happening next. Communication isn't a courtesy — it's part of how the work actually gets done well.

  4. iv.

    I treat the relationship as a partnership.

    Your input matters. Your time matters. Your dignity matters. The work happens with you, not to you.
